Before your exam

Choose your VEC exam instructions

WEARC conducts online amateur radio exams through two Volunteer Examiner Coordinators: ARRL VEC and SANDARC VEC. The fees and equipment requirements depend on the VEC for your specific session.

Which VEC should I choose? Check the VEC listed for the session you booked on HamStudy or in your registration confirmation. Your session determines the VEC, not your personal preference. If unclear, contact WEARC before paying or preparing equipment.
ARRL VEC

ARRL exam instructions

Use WEARC's established ARRL guides, including the two-device camera setup and exam payment.

  • Cameras: Two camera-equipped devices.
  • Exam fee: $15 standard; $5 under age 18.
  • Payment: Due within one hour of registration.

SANDARC VEC

SANDARC exam instructions

Read WEARC's separate SANDARC guide based on SANDARC's official VEC manual, not the existing ARRL camera or payment instructions.

  • Cameras: One camera by default, a second only if necessary.
  • Exam fee: Free for every candidate.
  • Procedure: One active screen, screen sharing, a room scan, no recording.

Read SANDARC exam guide

Do not pay through WEARC's ARRL payment page for a SANDARC session.

A separate $35 FCC application fee generally applies to a new license regardless of the VEC. A routine license-class upgrade does not incur that FCC fee.
