About WEARC

About the WEARC Online VE Team

Established in 1994, WEARC is the West Essex Amateur Radio Club. Our experienced volunteer examiner team conducts online amateur radio license exam sessions for Technician, General, and Amateur Extra candidates. We focus on clear instructions, calm pacing, and helping new operators get licensed and get on the air. We are located in Essex County, New Jersey approximately 15 miles West of New York City.

After the exam, candidates are welcome to connect with WEARC for mentoring, club activities, and help getting started in amateur radio.

ARRL VEC

Questions and answers for ARRL VEC candidates.

Do I need to live in New Jersey?

No. Our remote sessions welcome candidates throughout the United States and internationally. International candidates should review the additional FCC and ARRL VEC requirements below before registering.

Can international candidates take a U.S. Amateur Radio license exam with WEARC?

Yes. International candidates are welcome and encouraged to register with our team. U.S. citizenship and U.S. residence are not required. Under 47 CFR § 97.5(b)(1), a person who qualifies by examination may apply for an FCC amateur operator/primary station license except a representative of a foreign government.

Before registering, please review these requirements:

  • U.S. mailing address: ARRL VEC requires foreign applicants to provide a valid U.S. mailing address for the license application. See ARRL's guidance for foreign applicants. Current 47 CFR § 97.23 separately requires the license record to show the grantee's correct name, mailing address, and email address.
  • FCC FRN and email: An FRN and valid email address are required. A foreign applicant who is not eligible for a U.S. Social Security Number can register in FCC CORES as foreign/exempt and receive an FRN. See the ARRL 605 instructions and our FRN guide.
  • Phone number: ExamTools may not properly capture some international phone numbers. If the registration form will not accept your number, you may leave the phone field blank, but please email hamradiotest@osi3.net with a valid cell phone number, including country code, that can receive voice calls and text messages.
  • Photo ID: A government-issued passport is acceptable identification. Your registration name should match your ID.
  • NCVEC Form 605 certifications: All applicants must complete the required certifications and Basic Qualification Question. The 605 specifically requires the applicant to certify that they are not a representative of a foreign government. See the current NCVEC Form 605.

For the complete international-candidate guidance, see our online exam FAQ.

Can I take more than one exam element?

Yes. Email us ahead of time so we can plan enough time for you during the session.

I registered but did not get a confirmation email. What now?

Confirmation emails typically arrive within a few minutes. Check your spam folder first. If it is still missing, email us and we will help.

How do I reach the team?

Email hamradiotest@osi3.net. You can also call or text Mike at +1-917-502-2203.

What ID do I need for an online ham radio exam?

Most candidates can use one acceptable legal photo ID. ARRL also provides non-photo ID alternatives when no photo ID is available, plus a special identification rule for students and minors. See the WEARC online exam ID requirements for the complete list.

Is an FRN required for ham radio exam registration?

Yes. You need an FCC FRN to register for an amateur radio license exam. If you do not have one yet, register for an FRN online before the session.

What is the ARRL VEC ham radio exam fee?

ARRL VEC sessions only: The exam fee is $15 per session, or $5 for candidates under 18. After passing an element, you may take the next higher element(s) in the same session at no additional exam fee through Amateur Extra. Email us in advance if you plan to take multiple elements.

New licenses require a separate $35 FCC application fee paid directly to the FCC. A normal license-class upgrade does not require that fee. Eligible new-license candidates under 18 can apply for ARRL reimbursement after their license is issued. See our payment page and youth guide.

SANDARC VEC

Questions and answers for SANDARC VEC candidates.

Can I test with SANDARC from outside New Jersey?

Yes. WEARC conducts SANDARC VEC online exams remotely. Check the HamStudy session listing and contact us before booking if you have special eligibility or documentation questions.

Can international candidates register for a SANDARC session?

International candidates must meet FCC licensing requirements and have a valid FRN. If you cannot register an FRN through the normal FCC process, contact FCC support and our team before scheduling.

Can I take another exam element if I pass?

Yes, if the VE team can accommodate an additional element during your appointment.

What if I did not receive a confirmation email?

Check your spam folder. If your HamStudy or ExamTools confirmation is missing, contact the WEARC team for assistance.

How do I contact the WEARC exam team?

Email hamradiotest@osi3.net. You can also call or text +1-917-502-2203.

What identification do I need for a SANDARC remote exam?

Adults should have a government-issued photo ID. Candidates under 18 without their own photo ID must bring both a parent or guardian with photo ID and a separate document bearing the minor’s name, such as a school ID or library card. Other name-bearing documents are subject to the Team Lead’s approval; contact WEARC in advance about alternatives. The guardian’s ID and verbal verification alone are not sufficient.

Is an FCC FRN needed for a SANDARC exam?

Yes. Applicants need an FCC Registration Number before registering on HamStudy. Use your existing FRN if you already have one.

What does a SANDARC exam cost?

SANDARC VEC exams are free for every candidate. The FCC separately charges $35 for a new amateur license application, but not a routine license-class upgrade.
