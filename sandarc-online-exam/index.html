SANDARC online ham radio exam instructions

These instructions are for WEARC online exams coordinated by SANDARC VEC. One camera is standard, and the exam is free. Please read all requirements before your appointment.

Confirm your exam session Questions? Contact WEARC
Confirm your session: Your HamStudy registration should identify SANDARC VEC. Review the session confirmation before preparing for your exam, and contact WEARC if you have questions.
Step 1 • Registration

Register on HamStudy and bring your FRN

  1. Choose a WEARC session listed under SANDARC VEC on HamStudy. Complete the registration and ExamTools application questions.
  2. Have your 10-digit FCC Registration Number (FRN). Anyone with an existing FCC license already has one; never create a second FRN. New applicants can register through FCC CORES.
  3. Use your legal name exactly as shown on your identification and FCC record, and provide a working email address. Answer the FCC Basic Qualification (felony) question accurately.
  4. Watch for your HamStudy/ExamTools email with the Zoom meeting link and session instructions. Check your spam folder if necessary; contact us if it is missing.
Under age 13? Before registering, a parent or guardian must sign and email the ExamTools COPPA Parental Consent Form to coppa@examtools.org. ExamTools must process the consent before registration, not on exam day. The parent or guardian should plan to attend. Ask WEARC early if help locating the form is needed.
Step 2 • Equipment

One camera and one active screen

  • One camera is the standard. Your device must have a working webcam, microphone, and speaker. A second camera is used only when necessary, not as a routine requirement.
  • A computer or laptop is recommended for Zoom screen sharing and ExamTools.
  • Use reliable Internet, install/update Zoom, and test the camera, microphone, speaker and screen sharing before the session.
  • Use only one active monitor or screen. Additional monitors must be disconnected or covered. No virtual or blurred backgrounds.
  • No headphones or earbuds are permitted; use the device speaker and microphone.
  • Close unrelated software and be ready to share your screen. Examiners must check for unauthorized applications.
Step 3 • Exam space

A quiet room and a clear desk

  • Choose a quiet space with no interruptions or visible study aids.
  • Be ready for a slow 360-degree room scan with your camera, including the desk, nearby floor, and walls. Notes, posters, and whiteboards will be checked.
  • On your desk, keep only the computer, keyboard, and mouse, unless an accommodation has been arranged.
  • No physical calculator is permitted for remote SANDARC exams. Put away books, notes and study materials.
Step 4 • Identity check

Have acceptable identification ready

Adults should show a government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, state ID, or military ID). The legal name should match your FRN and ExamTools application. Examiners will check your ID visually over camera; no ID is photographed, recorded, or copied.

For candidates under 18 without a government-issued photo ID, a parent or guardian may show their own photo ID and verify the candidate's identity. A school or library ID may be accepted at the Team Lead's discretion.

Have your existing amateur license or a qualifying CSCE ready if claiming element credit or taking an upgrade. Ask WEARC ahead of time about unusual documentation.

Step 5 • During your exam

What happens on Zoom

  1. Join Zoom as directed by your registration email. The VEs will visually check your identification, conduct the room scan and ask you to share your screen.
  2. Open ExamTools and follow the exam instructions. At least three accredited VEs will observe throughout. Remain visible, connected and focused on your exam.
  3. When you complete the exam, ExamTools grades it. If you pass and time allows, you may be able to attempt the next element.
  4. If you do not pass, an immediate retest is at the VE team's discretion, not guaranteed. A new exam version is used, and a retest carries no SANDARC fee.
  5. Confirm your information and sign the required documents under VE direction. ExamTools issues a CSCE (Certificate of Successful Completion of Examination) for passed elements once the session is finalized.

If your video connection drops, contact the team promptly. A prolonged outage may invalidate an exam attempt.

Fees and licensing

SANDARC exam fee: $0

All SANDARC exams are free, including permitted retests. There is no WEARC exam fee to pay for your SANDARC session.

The FCC separately charges a $35 fee for a new amateur license application. When a passing application reaches the FCC, the FCC emails payment instructions. Pay at FCC CORES within 10 calendar days to avoid dismissal. Routine class upgrades do not incur this FCC application fee.

New licensees cannot transmit until their grant appears in FCC ULS. An already-licensed applicant who upgrades may use their CSCE to operate at the new privileges immediately, with the appropriate temporary call-sign identifier (such as /AG or /AE).

Check your FCC application status by FRN.

Other considerations

Accommodations and special situations

Accessibility: If you need an accommodation for a disability, please contact WEARC in advance.

Felony question: SANDARC VEs will not ask about the circumstances. If you answer “Yes” to the FCC Basic Qualification question, SANDARC directs you to submit an explanation with your FCC application number to attach605@fcc.gov within 14 days after the application is submitted.

International applicants: A valid FRN and complete FCC application are required. For applicants unable to register an FRN normally, SANDARC directs them to FCC support for alternate registration. Contact WEARC in advance if you have questions about eligibility or documentation.
