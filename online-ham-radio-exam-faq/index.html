Frequently asked questions

1. Before you register

Do I need to live in New Jersey?

No. Our online sessions welcome candidates throughout the United States and internationally. International candidates should review the additional FCC and ARRL VEC requirements below before registering.

Can international candidates take a U.S. Amateur Radio license exam with WEARC?

Yes. We welcome and encourage international candidates to register for our online sessions. U.S. citizenship and U.S. residence are not required. Under 47 CFR § 97.5(b)(1), a person who qualifies by examination may apply for an FCC amateur operator/primary station license except a representative of a foreign government.

U.S. mailing address

ARRL VEC requires foreign applicants to provide a valid U.S. mailing address for the license application. ARRL's current foreign-applicant guidance states that a U.S. mailing address is required for application purposes. Current 47 CFR § 97.23 separately requires every license record to show the grantee's correct name, mailing address, and email address.

FCC FRN and email address

An FCC Registration Number (FRN) and a valid email address are mandatory. If you are a foreign applicant who is not eligible for a U.S. Social Security Number, ARRL's 605 instructions explain that you can register online in FCC CORES as exempt by selecting the reason that the individual is foreign. An FRN will then be issued. See our FRN guide for the normal registration flow.

International phone numbers and ExamTools

ExamTools may not properly capture some international phone numbers. If the phone field will not accept your number, it is OK to leave that field blank. Please then email the WEARC VE Team at hamradiotest@osi3.net with a valid cell phone number, including country code, that can receive both voice calls and text messages.

Identification

You must present acceptable identification just like any other candidate. ARRL lists a government-issued passport as an acceptable legal photo ID. The name used for your FCC/ExamTools registration should match your identification.

NCVEC Form 605 certification and qualification question

All candidates must complete the required NCVEC Form 605 certifications and answer the FCC Basic Qualification Question. Of particular importance to an international applicant, the current NCVEC Form 605 requires the applicant to certify that they are not a representative of a foreign government. This matches the eligibility rule in 47 CFR § 97.5(b)(1). If you cannot make that certification truthfully, you are not eligible for an FCC amateur operator/primary station license.

After you are licensed

An FCC license does not automatically authorize you to transmit from another country. When operating outside areas regulated by the FCC, you must follow the rules and licensing or reciprocal-operating requirements of the country where the station is located. See ARRL International Operating.

Is an FCC FRN required?

Yes. Get your FRN before the session. Use our FRN how to guide to register.

What is the exam fee?

The fee is $15 per session and covers any higher element you pass during that session.

2. After you register

I did not get a confirmation email. What now?

Check your spam folder. If it is still missing, email hamradiotest@osi3.net.

What should I bring on exam day?

Bring a valid photo ID, your FRN, and any CSCEs. See the full what to bring checklist.

How do I prepare my workspace for the online exam?

Carefully review the online exam session requirements checklist.

What if my tech setup is not working?

Join early and use the troubleshooting checklist to fix audio, video, and screen sharing.

3. During and after your exam

Can I take multiple elements?

Yes. Email us ahead of time so we can reserve enough time during your session.

What happens after I pass?

Review the after you pass guide for FCC processing steps and use the FRN status lookup to see where your application appears to be.
